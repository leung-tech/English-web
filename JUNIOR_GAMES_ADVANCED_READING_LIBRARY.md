# P1–P3 Games and P4–P6 Advanced Reading Library

## P1–P3: Interactive foundational games

The junior-primary games remain short, repeatable and language-focused. They use familiar school and daily-life vocabulary, a clear answer action and immediate encouraging feedback.

| Year | Game 1 | Game 2 | Main language focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Phonics Pop · 拼音碰碰**: hear a familiar word and choose its initial sound | **Word Match · 單字配對**: reveal and match a word to a short clue | Initial sounds; colours, animals, classroom objects and toys |
| P2 | **Sound & Word Hunt · 聲音找字**: hear a word and identify its spelling | **Word Match · 單字配對**: match daily-life words to definitions | Daily routines, food, places, weather and body parts |
| P3 | **Story Clue Hunt · 故事線索遊戲**: choose the right story detail after a short audio line | **Word Match · 單字配對**: match experience words to meanings | Past-time words, jobs, transport, hobbies and feelings |

> Each game uses six compact items or three matching pairs. Pupils can replay audio, try again immediately and earn a clear completion result without time pressure.

## P4–P6: Advanced multi-genre reading workshop

The senior reading workshop adds a distinct genre label, two comprehension questions and a transparent model analysis for every passage. Analyses identify the evidence, show a concise model answer and explain the reading strategy.

| Year | Genre 1 | Genre 2 | Reading skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| P4 | School news report: Green Day success | Informal email: class museum invitation | Main idea, stated detail, writer purpose and text features |
| P5 | Advice leaflet: typhoon readiness | Travel blog: choosing a city walk | Imperatives, audience, comparison, inference and supporting detail |
| P6 | Magazine article: youth volunteer service | Formal letter: safer journey to school | Viewpoint, tone, evidence, proposal and pronoun reference |

## Model-analysis structure

Each question reveals the following only after the answer is checked.

| Layer | Student-facing prompt |
|---|---|
| **Find the clue · 找出線索** | The exact phrase or sentence that provides evidence. |
| **Model answer · 範例答案** | A short complete answer using the question wording. |
| **Why it works · 解題原因** | A concise explanation of the reading skill, such as matching detail, making an inference or identifying purpose. |

## Reading-practice expectations

P4 passages are approximately 90–120 words and focus on locating clearly stated details. P5 passages are approximately 120–150 words and ask pupils to compare information or infer an audience-focused point. P6 passages are approximately 150–180 words and include viewpoint, tone or evidence-based inference. The materials are original practice texts designed for the website and not reproduced examination papers.
