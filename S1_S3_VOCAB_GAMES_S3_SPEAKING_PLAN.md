# S1–S3 Vocabulary Games and S3 Speaking Simulation Plan

## Game progression

| Stage | Game | Language focus | Student action |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 Extend | **Phrase Builder** | School and community collocations, polite request frames and practical instructions. | Choose a fitting word or phrase to complete a meaningful sentence. |
| S2 Consolidate | **Meaning in Context** | Evidence, viewpoint and media-literacy phrases. | Choose the phrase that matches a purpose, claim or source situation. |
| S3 Ready | **Precision Challenge** | Critical-thinking collocations, qualification frames and evidence-led recommendation language. | Select the most precise expression, not simply a grammatically possible one. |

Each game will use the existing multiple-choice answer, hint, feedback, replay and browser-local progress controls. A dedicated game renderer will add a short phrase bank and a score-aware progress label without collecting personal data.

## S3 speaking simulation framework

The simulations are **original practice scenarios** modelled on common school oral-learning tasks, not official examination papers. Students receive a preparation role card, time guidance, a model response, targeted language frames and a transparent analytic rubric. They rehearse, self-check and can ask a teacher or parent to use the same rubric. No automated speech or language-quality score is claimed.

| Simulation | Interaction | Assessment focus |
|---|---|---|
| School improvement discussion | Recommend, listen and respond to a concern. | Relevant ideas, evidence, interaction and clarity. |
| Community proposal presentation | Present a proposal from a source pack, then answer a follow-up. | Organisation, evidence use, response and language control. |
| Source-evaluation conversation | Judge a claim, identify a limitation and suggest a next step. | Critical reasoning, precision, support and turn-taking. |

The rubric uses four transparent descriptors: **Ideas and Evidence**, **Organisation and Interaction**, **Language and Vocabulary**, and **Speech Clarity and Delivery**. It is a descriptive practice rubric only and is not presented as an official marking scheme.
