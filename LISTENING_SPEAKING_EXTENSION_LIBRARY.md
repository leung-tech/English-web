# P4–P6 Listening and Speaking Extension Library

## Learning sequence

Each senior-primary topic follows a short, reusable sequence: **preview key vocabulary, listen for a purpose, complete a quick check, and use the language in a role-play**. Vocabulary cards are not a word list to memorise in isolation. Every card comes from the listening scripts that pupils hear in the listening-comprehension sessions.

| Year | Vocabulary focus | Listening quick-check focus | Role-play setting |
|---|---|---|---|
| P4 | Recycling, healthy choices and clear school announcements | Identify a place, purpose, item and healthy choice after replaying a short extract | Eco Club invitation; Healthy Week interview |
| P5 | Safety, travel planning and comparison language | Identify advice, source of information, comparative reason and decision | Typhoon-safety call; Travel-forum planning |
| P6 | Community service, school transition and organised advice | Identify speaker purpose, action-plan detail, helpful response and inference | Volunteer Day planning; Secondary-school visitor conversation |

## Flashcard design

Every level provides eight cards. The **front** shows an English word or phrase and a short spoken prompt. The **back** reveals the Chinese meaning, a pupil-friendly definition and a context sentence. Pupils can use **I know this · 我已掌握** to track the words they know in the current browser.

| Level | Sample target language |
|---|---|
| P4 | announcement, recyclable, sort, reusable, healthy habit, fizzy drink, instead, active |
| P5 | typhoon, shoreline, official announcement, suspended, timetable, ferry, coach, recommend |
| P6 | volunteer, local need, action plan, reflection, independently, nervous, community, responsibility |

## Listening quick checks

Each level contains six replayable mini-items. Pupils hear a short extract or key sentence, choose the answer, and receive immediate bilingual feedback. Items are deliberately short so pupils can practise a specific listening action: recognising a **time/place**, **purpose**, **reason**, **instruction**, **comparison** or **speaker intention**.

> Pupils should look at the question first, listen for the relevant detail, and only then choose an answer. They may replay the audio before checking.

## Role-play dialogues

Each year level contains two role-plays with roles A and B, a clear goal, a model dialogue, language support and a self-check. Pupils may play both roles alone or practise with a partner.

| Year | Dialogue 1 | Dialogue 2 |
|---|---|---|
| P4 | Invite a friend to Eco Club Green Day | Ask and answer questions in a Healthy Week interview |
| P5 | Give typhoon-safety advice to a classmate | Compare transport choices for a school visit |
| P6 | Plan a Volunteer Day activity as a team | Ask a student helper for secondary-school advice |

## Safe oral-practice approach

This extension uses **sample role-play dialogues** rather than automated pronunciation scoring. It provides useful practice without collecting a child’s voice or making unreliable pronunciation claims. Each role-play is replayable through the browser’s English voice feature and asks pupils to assess clear voice, complete response, appropriate pace and a relevant follow-up question.
