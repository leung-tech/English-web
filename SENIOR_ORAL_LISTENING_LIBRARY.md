# P4–P6 Listening and Oral Practice Library

## Delivery approach

Listening passages use the browser’s built-in English speech playback. Pupils should read the question first, replay the passage as needed, select an answer, and then read the transcript after checking. Speaking prompts use a model delivery, a structured planning frame and a self-check. They are designed for practise rather than automatic pronunciation scoring.

## P4: Listening comprehension and oral presentation

| Module | Script or speaking task | Main skills |
|---|---|---|
| Listening 1: Eco Club announcement | The Eco Club meets on Thursday in Room 204. Pupils bring a clean plastic bottle and make pencil holders for Green Day. | Find time, place, item and purpose. |
| Listening 2: Healthy Week interview | A pupil explains cycling twice, walking with her father and drinking water instead of fizzy drinks. | Identify completed actions and one healthy choice. |
| Oral 1: My Healthy Week | A 45–60 second presentation with an opening, two habits and a closing suggestion. | Use *first*, *also* and *because*. |
| Oral 2: Our Green Day | Invite classmates to a recycling activity and explain what to bring. | Use future plans and clear sequence words. |

## Oral-presentation planning sequence

| Stage | Student action | Useful language |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | Greet the audience and name the topic | `Good morning. Today I would like to talk about...` |
| Main point 1 | Give a first point plus a reason or example | `First, ... because ...` |
| Main point 2 | Add a second point and make a link | `Another important point is...` |
| Closing | Restate the key message or call to action | `To sum up, ...` / `Let us ...` |

> **Speak from a plan, not from a memorised paragraph.** Pupils should replace the bracketed content with their own ideas, pause at full stops and use a clear pace.
