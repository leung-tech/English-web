# Hong Kong Senior Secondary English Alignment Research

## Purpose and scope

This note records the official sources used to calibrate the new S4–S6 Senior English Studio. The website materials are **original practice**. They do not reproduce examination questions, do not constitute an official HKDSE paper or marking scheme, and do not predict examination results.

## Key findings used in the design

The Education Bureau lists the **CDC-HKEAA English Language Curriculum and Assessment Guide (Secondary 4–6)** among its English Language Education curriculum documents. The Hong Kong Examinations and Assessment Authority identifies the same guide as the curriculum and assessment guide for HKDSE English Language. The HKEAA Assessment Frameworks page provides year-specific English Language frameworks, including 2026–2029 links. These official pages support the decision to use a distinct senior-secondary layer and to present it as curriculum-aligned original practice rather than as copied examination content.

The S4–S6 Senior English Studio therefore develops transferable language and literacy capabilities rather than claiming to replicate the official examination. Its first release focuses on advanced sentence control, evidence-aware reading and response, and purposeful writing for varied audiences and genres. In particular, students practise cohesive argument, precise vocabulary, formal register, source use, counterargument and rebuttal, while draft tasks remain self-checking only and do not provide automated language-quality scores.

## Implementation boundaries

| Website element | Alignment purpose | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Advanced grammar and sentence control | Supports sophisticated language use in formal and academic contexts. | Original items only; no official answers or mark allocation. |
| Critical reading and source-response tasks | Supports inference, viewpoint comparison, tone, rhetorical effect and evidence handling. | Original source packs and texts; not a reproduction of past-paper passages. |
| Writing scaffolds | Supports audience, purpose, organisation, content development and language choices. | Student drafts are stored locally; no automated quality score or official standard claimed. |
| S6 strategy label | Signals higher demand and preparation relevance. | Not an examination prediction, official sample, marking scheme or guaranteed outcome. |

## Paper 2 self-review and oral-practice boundary — 2026 update

The HKEAA English Language FAQ states that Paper 2 marking guidelines form the basis for both writing tasks but can be adapted to task requirements; it also explains that examination markers make a **best-fit** judgement across domains and that marking is distinct from final grading.[4] The FAQ further describes word guidance as support for task fulfilment, organisation and time management rather than a standalone quality score.[4]

Accordingly, the Senior English Studio Paper 2 tool provides **original self-review prompts** on task fulfilment, organisation, language control and revision. It does not generate an official mark, level, grade or claim to apply the HKEAA best-fit judgement. Its Group Interaction and Individual Response simulations are likewise original practice prompts with self-review rubrics, not official examination speaking tasks or assessment results.
