# S1–S3 Grammar Quest and Question-Bank Expansion Plan

## Purpose

This expansion increases the number of **original, contextual grammar questions** across S1–S3 and adds two targeted S1 Grammar Quest stages. The content remains original practice rather than an official examination or textbook replacement. Every objective item has one defined answer, a short English explanation, immediate Traditional Chinese support and browser-local feedback.

| Level | New module | Main grammar range | New question-bank baseline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 Extend** | Passive Voice Quest; Conditionals Quest; Grammar in School and Community | present and past passive voice; zero and first conditional; past continuous; future forms; relative clauses; gerund/infinitive; present perfect; comparison and modality | 24 game rounds plus 18 contextual grammar questions |
| **S2 Consolidate** | Grammar in Evidence and Viewpoints | passive with agent; reported language; first and second conditional; present perfect; relative clauses; participle use; purpose, concession, quantifiers and careful claims | 18 contextual grammar questions |
| **S3 Ready** | Grammar in Formal Response | passive reporting; clause reduction; participles; second and mixed conditional; formal register; nominalisation; hedging; parallelism; linking and relative clauses | 18 contextual grammar questions |

## Question-format rotation

The interface will label each objective question so students meet varied prompts rather than only one type of gap fill.

| Label | Student action |
|---|---|
| **Complete the message** | Select the grammar form that completes an authentic school, community or formal message. |
| **Choose the best correction** | Identify the most accurate revision of an error-prone sentence. |
| **Transform the notice** | Choose the sentence that keeps the original meaning while changing grammar form. |
| **Check the sentence** | Select the grammatically accurate sentence for the context. |
| **Use the clue** | Apply a time, purpose, condition or evidence clue to decide the form. |
| **Edit for a formal response** | Choose the most precise grammar for a proposal, report or source-based response. |

## S1 quest stages

| Stage | Round count | Learning outcome |
|---|---:|---|
| **Passive Voice Quest: What happens at school?** | 12 | Identify action receivers, form be + past participle, choose an appropriate tense, and use passive notices accurately. |
| **Conditionals Quest: Plan, predict and solve** | 12 | Distinguish general rules from likely future results and choose zero or first conditional forms for school and community actions. |

## Quality boundaries

The website will automatically score only the objective questions with defined answers. Writing and speaking completion can be recorded locally, but neither grammar quality nor an examination level is generated automatically. Progress stays in the current browser and can be cleared by the student.
