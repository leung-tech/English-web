# S1–S3 Interaction, Grammar and Critical Thinking Extension Plan

## Learning progression

The extension keeps every item as original practice, with English as the learning target and Traditional Chinese as immediate support. S1 interaction focuses on asking clearly, negotiating simple practical solutions and responding politely. S2 interaction adds evidence, viewpoint comparison and collaborative decision-making. S3 interaction requires source evaluation, qualification of claims, prioritising feedback and defensible recommendations.

| Year | Added dialogue and speaking emphasis | Added grammar emphasis | Writing and thinking emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Clarifying instructions, planning events, resolving shared-space issues and contributing to a group discussion. | Countability, subject–verb agreement, present/past contrasts, modals, linkers, indirect questions and first conditional. | Identify a problem, explain a reason and propose one realistic step. |
| S2 | Comparing options, responding to competing needs, discussing reliability and negotiating a joint action. | Present perfect, passive voice, relative clauses, reporting verbs, purpose, contrast and conditional language. | Separate claim from evidence, compare perspectives and make a balanced recommendation. |
| S3 | Challenging an overgeneralisation respectfully, evaluating source quality, revising from feedback and defending a proposal. | Hedging, reported speech, passive forms, advanced linkers, conditionals, modal certainty and cohesion. | Evaluate evidence, identify limitations, distinguish fact from interpretation and justify an evidence-led recommendation. |

## New data and interface structure

A new editable data file will provide three compact extension banks: `S1_INTERACTION_PLUS`, `S2_INTERACTION_PLUS` and `S3_CRITICAL_PLUS`. The existing S1 Extend, S2 Consolidate and S3 Ready stages will each receive three additional module cards: a grammar clinic, an interaction dialogue lab and a structured speaking studio. S3 will additionally receive a second advanced-writing module containing two source packs.

| Extension bank | Grammar | Speaking | Dialogue | Advanced writing |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| S1 Interaction Plus | 12 contextual questions | 4 structured tasks | 3 role-plays with checkpoints | — |
| S2 Interaction Plus | 12 contextual questions | 4 structured tasks | 3 role-plays with checkpoints | — |
| S3 Critical Plus | 12 contextual questions | 4 structured tasks | 3 role-plays with checkpoints | 2 source-pack tasks |

The existing dialogue engine will continue to provide replay, selectable reply checkpoints, bilingual explanations and browser-local completion marking. Speaking stays self-guided: learners hear a model, adapt one detail, and self-check; the platform makes no automated speech-quality claim. The two S3 writing packs provide evidence, limitations, counterpoints, paragraph maps and language frames, while completion remains a word-count and self-check process rather than an automated score.
