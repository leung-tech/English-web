# P1–P4 Grammar and S1–S3 Reading/Writing Expansion Audit

## Purpose

This internal course map records the next original-practice expansion for the Primary English Studio and Secondary English Studio. It treats the existing materials as learning practice rather than official school assessment, examination material, or score prediction. Objective grammar and reading tasks may give immediate feedback. Writing tasks remain guided practice with browser-local drafting and self-check only.

## P1–P4 grammar review

The current P1–P4 bank already includes basic practice in present continuous forms, demonstratives and possessives at P1; habitual present, plural noun phrases, time prepositions and present continuous at P2; past/future forms, movement prepositions and comparatives at P3; and near-future plans, obligation, superlatives and time connectors at P4. The review identified an opportunity to increase deliberate practice of the foundational contrasts that students need before P5–P6: subject–verb agreement, articles, pronouns, punctuation in questions, simple-present negatives/questions, countable and uncountable nouns, past-tense irregular forms, future-plan choice, comparatives/superlatives, modal meanings and conjunction use.

| Year | Existing emphasis | Additional interactive focus |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | *be*, present continuous, demonstratives and possessives | Subject pronouns, *a/an*, singular/plural nouns, *can/can’t*, basic place prepositions, capital letters and question marks. |
| P2 | Daily routines, time words, plurals and present continuous | *do/does* questions, *don’t/doesn’t*, countable/uncountable nouns, *some/any*, *there is/are*, frequency adverbs and time prepositions. |
| P3 | Simple past/future, comparatives and movement prepositions | Regular/irregular past, *will* versus *be going to*, comparative/superlative choice, conjunctions, object pronouns and *when/if* time or condition clauses. |
| P4 | Future forms, obligation, superlatives and instructions | Simple-present/past/future choice, *should/must/have to*, comparative/superlative spelling, *because/so/although*, prepositions of time, and short relative clauses with *who/which*. |

The implementation adds eight original contextual four-option grammar checks per grade. The combined P1–P4 grammar bank will therefore provide at least twelve current coverage-extension checks per grade, alongside the base bank and other existing practice modules.

## S1–S3 advanced reading and writing design

The secondary studio already has broad reading, writing, vocabulary, listening and language-use coverage. The planned additions strengthen cross-source reasoning, audience-aware response and formal organisation with a separate advanced reading-and-writing resource at each stage.

| Stage | Advanced reading resource | Advanced writing resource |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | A school-community announcement and pupil comment about a shared study space. Learners identify purpose, evidence, differing views, reference, reasonable inference, connector function, tone and a balanced next step. | A 120–150 word article for new S1 pupils about using a shared study space responsibly. The scaffold uses an audience-aware opening, a point/example/reason paragraph, one consideration for others and a practical ending. |
| S2 | Two contrasting original sources about a later library opening: a student survey extract and a staff notice. Learners compare claims, scope, limits, source purpose, word choice, implied concerns, connector logic and a cautious conclusion. | A 170–200 word formal report to a school committee. The scaffold integrates the two sources, distinguishes a finding from its limit, develops two PEEL-style observations and makes a reviewable recommendation. |
| S3 | Three original source extracts on a community transport trial: data note, student testimony and facilities response. Learners evaluate relevance, agreement, caveat, implicit position, reliability limit, rhetorical tone, evidence-based conclusion and wording precision. | A 220–260 word balanced article about a transport-trial decision. The scaffold incorporates all three sources, concedes a concern, develops an evidence-led rebuttal, uses at least one varied sentence opening and gives a qualified conclusion. |

Each reading resource supplies eight original English-first, Traditional-Chinese-supported objective checks. Each writing resource has a source pack, paragraph map, language bank, original model and self-check. The model functions only as a structure and language example; it is not a marking scheme or a guaranteed high-score answer.

## Quality controls

The primary audit will require the new P1–P4 grammar extension and eight valid bilingual questions per year. The secondary audit will load the new S1–S3 extension alongside the existing curriculum bank and require at least twenty-eight reading questions and three advanced-writing tasks at each stage. Automated syntax, content audits, local interaction checks, public GitHub Pages checks and version-control checks will be run before release.
