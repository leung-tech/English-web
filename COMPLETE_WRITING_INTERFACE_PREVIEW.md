# Complete P4–P6 Writing-Support Interface Preview

## Entry journey

A pupil selects **P4, P5 or P6**, opens **Write · 寫作與編輯**, then selects **High-score models · 呈分試高分範文**. The model library opens with year tabs, two genre choices per year, an English-first model composition and Chinese support for task instructions, genre labels and learning advice.

| Interface zone | What the pupil sees | Learning purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Model picker | P4–P6 tabs and two genre cards for each year | Choose an age-appropriate email, story, proposal or article model |
| Writing paper | Writing task, suggested word range and full model composition | Notice format, organisation and language choices in context |
| Marking lens | Five bilingual criteria: task fulfilment, content development, organisation, accuracy, vocabulary and variety | Make high-score expectations visible before drafting |
| Why it works | Three model-specific strengths | Connect the model to exam-writing decisions |
| Build from the model | Common mistakes, vocabulary builder, sentence patterns and quick correction quiz | Move from passive reading to guided language practice |

## P4 preview

P4 includes an **informal email** and a **picture story**. The email model guides pupils through subject lines, friendly invitations, future plans and complete reason clauses. The picture story guides them through simple past, past continuous, sequence linkers and feeling words. Its quiz completion state was tested with a displayed **2/3** practice result, restart control and feedback for an incorrect tense correction.

## P5 preview

P5 includes a **formal email** and a **narrative**. The formal-email model highlights respectful purpose statements, modal verbs, conditionals and developed benefits. The narrative model develops setting, past-tense storytelling, action details and reflective endings. The sentence-pattern panel provides three adaptable frames, while the scoring lens remains visible alongside the model.

## P6 preview

P6 includes a **proposal** and a **magazine article**. The proposal supports formal openings, modal passive voice and purposeful conditionals. The magazine article supports subject–verb agreement, base verbs after *can*, linkers, examples and calls to action. The active article quiz presents one weak sentence, three correction choices, bilingual instructions and immediate feedback after checking.
