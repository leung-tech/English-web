# S1 Completion and S2 English Module Plan

**Author:** Manus AI  
**Status:** Implementation blueprint  
**Scope:** Original, bilingual junior-secondary learning materials for Primary English Studio. This document is a practical learning-plan reference, **not** an official Education Bureau syllabus, school scheme of work or examination paper.

## 1. Scope and design boundary

The Education Bureau publishes English Language Education curriculum documentation across primary and secondary levels, while its Junior Secondary English Classroom resource supports reading and writing across the curriculum.[1] [2] The platform should therefore organise S1 learning around four language skills, language knowledge, communication in familiar contexts and diverse text types, rather than present a single universal school textbook sequence.

> **Design rule:** Every S1 or S2 item remains original, age-appropriate, English-first with Traditional Chinese support, and clearly labelled as practice. Individual schools may sequence grammar and set texts differently.

## 2. S1 core coverage matrix

| Strand | Current live coverage | Completion release | Core practice outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grammar in context | Subject–verb agreement, articles, countability, time/place prepositions, present routines, present continuous, connectors and modals | **S1 Grammar Extension**: past events, future plans, comparison, question forms, pronouns/determiners and paragraph cohesion | Select, repair and explain a sentence in a school-life context. |
| Vocabulary and word study | Twelve school-life words, including timetable, assignment, equipment and permission | **S1 Vocabulary Builder**: learning habits, people and places, feelings, digital learning and word families | Select a precise word and use it in a complete sentence. |
| Reading | Notice/information text and linked cloze; main idea, detail, inference and instruction reading | **S1 Reading Workshop**: profile, email, blog and poster/infographic texts; purpose, audience, reference and evidence | Locate a phrase that supports an answer and explain the choice. |
| Listening | Two replayable school announcement/planning-dialogue scripts with detail questions | **S1 Listening Lab**: timetable change, club interview, lost-property notice and student podcast | Predict, replay, retrieve a detail and check a short note. |
| Writing | P6-to-S1 transition article and generic writing practice | **S1 Writing Workshop**: informal email, short recount and school notice response with planning/self-check | Draft for a clear purpose, use paragraphing and check audience/register. |
| Speaking | Existing P6 presentation and role-play activities | **S1 Speaking Studio**: introduce a club, ask for clarification and give a short opinion with one reason | Speak from a prompt, replay a model and self-check clarity. |
| Study and assessment habits | Local review, parent/teacher tracking and progress rewards | **S1 Core Checkpoint**: a low-stakes mixed-skills review with feedback by skill tag | Identify one strength and one next practice route. |

## 3. S1 completion release order

| Release | Modules | Content target | Acceptance check |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **S1-A — Language and word choices** | Grammar Extension; Vocabulary Builder | 16 grammar questions; 18 vocabulary questions | Four options, answer/explanation, Chinese support, unique IDs and context tags. |
| **S1-B — Texts and listening** | Reading Workshop; Listening Lab | 4 new texts / 12 questions; 2 new scripts / 8 questions | Each text has genre/purpose; each listening task is replayable and transcript-supported. |
| **S1-C — Communication** | Writing Workshop; Speaking Studio | 3 writing tasks; 3 speaking tasks | Clear audience/purpose, planning prompt and self-check; no automatic quality claim. |
| **S1-D — Core Checkpoint** | Mixed review and routing | 20 tagged items | Results report skills practised and recommended next module, not a student label. |

## 4. S2 module architecture

S2 should develop complexity, not merely increase question length. It follows the S1 Core Path after all S1-A to S1-D modules have been released.

| S2 module | Language focus | Text and communication focus | First content package |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S2 Grammar: Experiences and Choices** | Present perfect vs simple past; `for`/`since`; modals; comparatives/superlatives | Travel, service, health and school-improvement contexts | 16 contextual grammar and editing items. |
| **S2 Reading: Compare and Connect** | Cohesion, reference, tone and vocabulary in context | Blog plus report; interview plus notice; two short related texts | 4 paired-text reading sets, 12 questions. |
| **S2 Vocabulary: Themes and Word Families** | Prefixes/suffixes, collocation and word choice | Environment, technology, health and community | 18 vocabulary-in-context questions. |
| **S2 Listening: Longer Conversations** | Note-taking for people, times, reasons and changes | School trip, community project, student interview and radio feature | 3 replayable scripts, 12 questions. |
| **S2 Writing and Speaking: Explain and Compare** | Paragraph linkers, examples and balanced opinions | Email/report paragraph, comparison paragraph and short discussion | 3 guided writing/speaking tasks with self-checks. |

## 5. Learning sequence and quality requirements

The S1 landing path should progress from **notice and routine → word choice → information text → listening detail → purposeful response**, then finish with a mixed checkpoint. The S2 path should progress from **experience and comparison → connected texts → longer listening → explanation and opinion**.

Every new item must use a stable ID, a stage/module tag, English prompt, Traditional Chinese support, a valid answer or self-check condition, and an explanation. Every reading text must name its genre and purpose. Every listening script must provide replay and transcript access. Writing and speaking tasks must never present a word-count or completion check as an automated language-quality score.
