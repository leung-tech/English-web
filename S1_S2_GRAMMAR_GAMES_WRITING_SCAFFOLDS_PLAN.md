# S1–S2 Grammar Challenges and Genre-writing Scaffolds Plan

## Grammar challenge progression

| Stage | Challenge | Language focus | Completion feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 Extend | **Grammar Quest: Build the Message** | be / have, articles, singular and plural nouns, present and past forms, prepositions, question word order, can/could and basic linkers. | Six rounds per checkpoint; each correct answer receives an explanation, and the local dashboard reports objective accuracy after three items. |
| S2 Consolidate | **Grammar Quest: Shape the Argument** | present perfect, past perfect, passive voice, relative clauses, reported speech, conditionals, modals, linkers and cautious evidence language. | Six rounds per checkpoint; feedback recommends explanation review, a related game or a clinic according to objective accuracy. |

The challenges use original school, community and media scenarios. They are not official examination papers. The question engine provides automated feedback only because each item has a defined answer.

## Writing scaffold progression

| Year | Genre | Writing purpose | Scaffold components |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Narrative: **An Unexpected Helpful Moment** | Tell a personal or school-community story clearly. | Story arc, setting/action/feeling prompts, time-sequence bank, sensory-detail choices, 90–120 word target, self-check. |
| S1 | Argument: **Should our class have a weekly screen-free activity?** | State a simple opinion, reasons and a practical conclusion. | Position/reason/example/counterpoint/close map, basic opinion linkers, 100–120 word target, self-check. |
| S2 | Narrative: **The Day Our Plan Changed** | Develop a chronological story with a problem, response and reflection. | Scene/problem/turning point/action/reflection map, past-tense editing cues, 120–150 word target, self-check. |
| S2 | Argument: **Should students help decide a school improvement?** | Build a balanced viewpoint with evidence and a response to one concern. | Claim/evidence/reason/counterpoint/recommendation map, balanced language bank, 120–150 word target, self-check. |

All writing tasks remain browser-local completion and planning tools. They do not automatically grade language quality, organisation, ideas, pronunciation or examination level.
