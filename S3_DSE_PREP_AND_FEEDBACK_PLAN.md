# S3 DSE-Preparation and Learning-Feedback Plan

## Scope and positioning

The new S3 material is **original senior-secondary / HKDSE-preparation practice** for S3 learners. It is not an official HKDSE paper, official marking scheme or score prediction. HKEAA publishes subject information and samples of candidates’ performance for English Language, while its English Language FAQ sets out assessment weighting by paper. [1] [2]

| Area | New learning support | Safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| Advanced grammar | 12 context-rich items on nominalisation, inversion, participle clauses, relative clauses, hedging, conditionals, cohesive devices, passive reporting and formal register. | Every item is original and provides an explanation plus bilingual support. |
| Advanced writing models | Two 200–250 word source-led models: a formal proposal and a discursive feature article. Each model includes a source pack, paragraph map, language bank and self-check. | Models are explicitly practice exemplars, not official scripts or official answers. |
| Objective-item feedback | Browser-local attempts, correct answers, accuracy by module, route and stage, plus a next-step message. | It reports practice evidence only; it is not a diagnostic, school record or predicted examination result. |
| Writing and speaking | Browser-local completion records, saved drafts, existing self-checks and descriptive rubrics. | No automated judgement of language quality, ideas, pronunciation, fluency or examination level. |

## Feedback rules

> **Automatic feedback applies only to questions with a defined correct answer.**

| Objective accuracy after at least 3 answered items | Feedback | Suggested next step |
|---|---|---|
| 75%–100% | Secure practice pattern | Attempt a new module or explain one answer aloud. |
| 50%–74% | Developing pattern | Revisit the explanation and replay/re-read the context. |
| Below 50% | Review needed | Use the hint, complete one related game or grammar clinic, then retry. |
| Fewer than 3 items | Too little evidence | Continue the module before drawing a conclusion. |

The dashboard will clearly state that all data remain on the device in `localStorage`; students can clear records and saved drafts at any time.
