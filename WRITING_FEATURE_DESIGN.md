# P4–P6 Assessment Writing Feature Design

## Entry point

A new **High-score models · 呈分試高分範文** card will appear in the Write route only when the selected year is P4, P5 or P6. This prevents junior-primary pupils from seeing advanced length and genre expectations too early.

## Library layout

The writing-model view is arranged as a focused assessment resource rather than a question-by-question exercise. A year selector shows **P4, P5 and P6**, and two model cards are available for each year. The selected model opens in a paper-style reading panel with the task, model composition and a concise word-count badge.

| Area | Purpose | Bilingual treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Grade selector | Switch between P4, P5 and P6 model sets | English year label with Chinese year support |
| Model picker | Choose a genre and topic | English genre and title, followed by Chinese genre label |
| Writing paper | Read the task and model answer | English composition stays visually primary; Chinese support identifies the task, genre and learning purpose |
| Score panel | Show transparent quality criteria | Each criterion has an English heading, Chinese explanation and a one-line high-score indicator |
| Learning notes | Explain what makes the model effective | “Why it works · 閱卷重點” links the composition to task fulfilment, development, organisation, accuracy and vocabulary |

## Marking guidance

The assessment panel uses five practical dimensions: task fulfilment, content development, organisation, language accuracy, and vocabulary and variety. These are presented as **self-review indicators**, not an official school-specific rubric. Pupils can compare their own draft against the model’s structure and use the scoring points as an editing checklist.

## Safety and learning approach

Every model carries a short reminder that it is a guide for structure and language choices, not a script to memorise. This supports original pupil writing while making high-quality features visible and teachable.
