# S2 Module 2 Blueprint: Messages and Media

**Module title:** S2 Connect: Messages and Media  
**Chinese title:** 中二連結：訊息與媒體  
**Status:** Approved for implementation as the second original S2 unit.  
**Boundary:** Every text, model, script and question will be original bilingual practice. It is not an official examination paper.

## Learning progression

The first S2 unit, **Experiences and Choices**, taught students to compare realistic options, explain experiences and support recommendations with evidence. This second unit shifts to a connected communication task: students identify who is speaking, judge a message’s purpose, report key information clearly and create an appropriate message for a real audience.

> **Module question:** How can students use accurate language to describe people and events, report what others have said, and share reliable information with an audience?

## Four-skills scope

| Route | Module name | Original content scope | Student output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apply | Grammar: Messages in context | 16 questions on relative clauses, passive voice and basic reported speech. | Complete a news, notice or message sentence accurately. |
| Apply | Vocabulary: Media messages | 18 words for news, reporting, digital information and responsible communication. | Select a word that fits a real communication context. |
| Read | Reading: Sources and voices | 4 paired-text sets / 12 questions. | Compare audience, purpose, fact, source and useful evidence. |
| Listen | Listening: Hear the message | 3 scripts / 12 questions. | Identify a speaker, purpose, key detail, source or next action. |
| Write | Writing: Inform an audience | 3 structured 100–120 word tasks. | Write a news report, formal invitation/update or responsible online notice. |
| Listen | Speaking: Report and respond | 3 model-supported 45–60 second tasks. | Report an event, distinguish fact from opinion, and give clear advice. |

## Language focus

| Section | Language focus | Contexts |
|---|---|---|
| **Relative clauses** | `who`, `which`, `that`, `where` to identify a person, place, event or object. | A school club member, a library service, an event venue and a campaign idea. |
| **Passive voice** | Present and past simple passive: `is held`, `are displayed`, `was organised`, `were collected`. | Notices, event reports, school news and community campaigns. |
| **Reported speech** | Basic `said that`, `told us that`, `asked us to` and tense/pronoun attention in short messages. | Interviews, announcements, feedback and class updates. |
| **Message quality** | Source, audience, purpose, fact, opinion, clear action and responsible sharing. | School media, community projects, digital-safety messages and cultural events. |

## Assessment-ready content quantities

The full release contains **64 learning items**: 16 grammar questions, 18 vocabulary items, 12 paired-reading questions, 12 listening questions, three writing tasks and three speaking tasks. Every objective item includes four options, an answer, an English explanation, Traditional Chinese support and a useful revision hint.

## Listening, writing and speaking tasks

The three listening scripts are a school podcast briefing, a community media-club meeting and a youth-radio digital-safety segment. Writing tasks ask learners to produce a short event report, a formal email updating participants and an online notice with responsible sharing advice. Speaking tasks ask learners to report an event, turn interview points into a summary, and respond clearly to a message whose reliability needs checking.

## Gemini multi-page integration

The unit will use the established standalone pattern. It will have one dedicated data file in `gemini-pages/data/` and six readable pages under `gemini-pages/s2/`: grammar, vocabulary, reading, listening, writing and speaking. The existing shared `lesson.css` and `lesson.js` files will be extended only where a unit-selection entry is required. This ensures Gemini can edit one content file without accidentally changing the whole platform.
