# Secondary English Content Planning — Research Notes

## Scope and positioning

The next content strand should be presented as **S1–S3 foundation and DSE-readiness skills**, not as official HKDSE practice. It will use original passages and questions while building transferable grammar, reading and writing skills.

## Official reference points

| Source | Relevant planning point |
|---|---|
| Education Bureau English Language Education curriculum documents | The EDB maintains curriculum documentation for both secondary and primary English Language Education, supporting a staged progression rather than a direct jump from P6 to senior-secondary work. |
| HKEAA English Language FAQs | The published weighting table distinguishes Paper 1, Paper 2, Paper 3, Paper 4 and School-based Assessment. This supports a long-term four-skills architecture, while the first build should concentrate on grammar-in-context and reading foundations. |
| HKEAA 2025 samples of candidates' performance | The HKEAA provides public samples for English Language Papers 1, 2 and 3, showing that reading, writing and listening/integrated skills have distinct assessment contexts. |

## Recommended content sequence

1. **S1 bridge:** sentence building, paragraph cohesion, vocabulary from school/community texts; inferencing with short notices, articles and profiles.
2. **S2 develop:** tense/aspect choices, clauses, relative pronouns, modals and text-based grammar; compare viewpoints and identify writer purpose.
3. **S3 prepare:** passive/reporting structures, conditionals, participle/infinitive patterns, editing; longer multi-text reading, argument and evidence selection.
4. **DSE foundation:** original Paper-1-style reading habits, Paper-2 purpose/audience/register planning, and later listening/integrated-skills preparation. Clearly label all content as foundation practice, not official HKDSE materials.
