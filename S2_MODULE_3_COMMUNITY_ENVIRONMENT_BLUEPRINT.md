# S2 Module 3 Content Blueprint: Community and Environment

## Unit identity

| Field | Specification |
|---|---|
| Unit label | **S2 Action: Community and Environment** |
| Chinese label | **中二行動：社區與環境** |
| Learning journey | Experiences and Choices → Messages and Media → evidence-led community action. |
| Practice status | Original bilingual practice; not an official examination paper. |
| Student audience | Hong Kong junior-secondary learners, accessed from the existing P6 transition and S2 practice routes. |

## Language pathway

The unit moves students from recognising a problem to proposing a realistic response. Grammar is introduced through community noticeboards, waste-reduction projects, refill stations, surveys and youth participation. Learners use quantity words accurately, describe resources, explain purpose, and state probable results with the first conditional.

| Skill | Focus | Minimum deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Grammar | `there is/are`; `some/any`; countable and uncountable nouns; `much/many/few/little`; `too/enough`; `to + verb` for purpose; first conditional. | 16 contextual four-option questions with bilingual explanations and hints. |
| Vocabulary | `access`, `collect`, `compost`, `conserve`, `contribute`, `dispose`, `facility`, `impact`, `participate`, `protect`, `reduce`, `refill`, `resource`, `reuse`, `survey`, `sustainable`, `volunteer`, `waste`. | 18 four-option word-in-context tasks with definition, example and Traditional Chinese meaning. |
| Paired reading | Compare reports, notices, proposals, posters, emails and survey summaries. | 4 two-text sets × 3 questions = 12 questions. |
| Listening | Hear actions, quantities, locations, reasons and next steps in school/community settings. | 3 replayable scripts × 4 questions = 12 questions. |
| Writing | Persuasive email, school proposal, community action article. | 3 original 100–120 word tasks with plan and self-check. |
| Speaking | Recommend a green action, compare project options, report a survey result. | 3 model-supported 45–60 second tasks with completion-only self-check. |

## Reading design

| Set | Text A | Text B | Target skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Refill Station | Project report | Student notice | Locate evidence and action instructions. |
| 2. Clean Shore Day | Volunteer diary | Event poster | Compare past experience with invitation details. |
| 3. Food-Waste Survey | Survey summary | Canteen proposal | Interpret quantities and select a supported solution. |
| 4. Neighbourhood Trees | Council message | Student email | Distinguish formal facts from a personal recommendation. |

## Listening design

| Script | Situation | Listening focus |
|---|---|---|
| Green Week briefing | A school coordinator assigns sustainable actions. | Roles, time, location and purpose. |
| Recycling Club meeting | Students plan a collection point and record quantities. | Checkable facts, resources and next steps. |
| Youth radio interview | A volunteer explains a community tree-care project. | Reasons, conditions and ways to take part. |

## Writing and speaking safeguards

Every writing and speaking activity must tell students that a completion record is **not** an automated language-quality score. It must show English first, give immediate Traditional Chinese support, store only local browser progress, and retain the Original Practice label.

## Acceptance checklist

The module is ready only when the original platform and Gemini standalone workspace both show six modules; all 64 items pass structural audit; all paired texts are responsive; listening replay works with browser speech synthesis; writing requires at least 100 words before completion; speaking provides a replayable model and self-check; and the public deployment is verified on GitHub Pages.
