## Cover

# P4–P6 Listening & Speaking Studio

### Replayable listening, confident speaking and guided oral practice
### 小四至小六聆聽與口語訓練模組

## Slide 1

# One pathway links listening to speaking

- **Preview key language** with listening vocabulary cards before the audio begins. 先預習重點詞彙，再開始聆聽。
- **Listen for a purpose** by identifying a place, reason, instruction, comparison or speaker intention. 帶着目的聆聽重點資料。
- **Use the same language aloud** in a structured presentation or role-play. 把聽到的語言轉化為有結構的口語表達。

## Slide 2

# Four modules create a complete practice cycle

| Module | Student action | Learning outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Listening lab · 聆聽練習室 | Replay longer scripts; answer comprehension questions | Main idea, details and inference |
| Listening vocabulary · 聆聽詞彙卡 | Reveal meaning; hear the word; say an example | Listening-ready topic vocabulary |
| Listening quick check · 聽後小測 | Replay a short extract; choose; check instantly | Focused listening accuracy |
| Oral practice · 口語練習 | Follow a plan or take a role | Clear, complete spoken responses |

## Slide 3

# P4 builds confidence through familiar school life

- **Listening contexts:** Eco Club announcements and Healthy Week interviews. 環保小組廣播與健康周訪問。
- **Vocabulary:** recyclable, reusable, healthy habit and instead. 可回收、可重用及健康生活詞彙。
- **Speaking:** a 45–60 second talk and partner dialogue using *First*, *I also* and *because*. 使用次序詞和理由表達。

## Slide 4

# P5 moves from detail to reasoned choices

- **Listening contexts:** typhoon-safety reports and travel-forum discussions. 颱風安全報告與旅遊討論。
- **Listening focus:** trusted source, safety advice, comparison and a final decision. 分辨可信資訊、安全建議、比較及結論。
- **Speaking:** give a recommendation, explain two reasons and respond using *However* and *Therefore*. 提出推薦、兩個理由和轉折回應。

## Slide 5

# P6 prepares pupils for independent communication

- **Listening contexts:** Volunteer Day planning and a secondary-school visit. 義工日規劃與中學參觀。
- **Listening focus:** community need, action-plan detail, reflection and inference. 社區需要、行動計劃、反思與推論。
- **Speaking:** deliver a 90–120 second proposal or give organised advice in a role-play. 完成 90–120 秒建議書演說或組織建議對話。

## Slide 6

# Interactive checks make listening visible

- **Replay first:** pupils can listen again before choosing an answer. 先重播，後作答。
- **Check immediately:** every quick-check item gives bilingual feedback and reveals the short transcript after marking. 即時中英回饋；核對後顯示逐字稿。
- **Review deliberately:** incorrect objective items are retained in the existing review list. 錯題保留在溫習清單，方便再挑戰。

## Slide 7

# Oral practice is structured, adaptable and safe

- **Presentation plans:** opening, point 1, point 2 and closing transform a topic into a clear talk. 四步框架把題目轉化成清晰短講。
- **Role-play dialogues:** pupils practise both A and B roles, then change details to make a response their own. 練習 A、B 角色，再替換細節建立個人回應。
- **No voice capture:** browser audio provides a model; pupils self-check clear voice, complete response and pace. 不收集學生聲音，以示範語音和自評培養信心。

## Slide 8

# A simple routine supports steady progress

- **Before listening:** complete 2–3 vocabulary cards. 聆聽前完成 2–3 張詞彙卡。
- **During listening:** replay the script and complete one quick-check set. 聆聽時重播內容並完成一組小測。
- **After listening:** give one short talk or role-play twice, improving one detail each time. 聆聽後完成短講或角色對話兩次，每次改善一個細節。
