# Junior Games and Advanced Reading Feature Design

## Junior-primary placement

The **Apply · 語言運用** route receives two P1–P3-only modules.

| Module | Interaction | Feedback |
|---|---|---|
| **Phonics & story game · 拼音與故事遊戲** | Pupils replay an audio word or short line, then choose one of four answers. | Immediate bilingual explanation, answer state and normal session progress. |
| **Word Match · 單字配對** | Pupils select a word, reveal a clear Chinese-supported definition and confirm it aloud. | A self-check completion action; no timer or penalty. |

The compact game activities use the existing session renderer, audio control, answer marking and review list. This gives P1–P3 a playful route without introducing a confusing new navigation system.

## Senior-primary placement

The **Read · 閱讀理解** route receives a P4–P6-only **Advanced reading workshop · 進階閱讀工作坊** module. It draws a short session from genre-labelled texts at the selected level.

| Screen layer | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Genre label | States whether the text is a news report, email, leaflet, travel blog, magazine article or formal letter. |
| Passage | Presents the original practice text in a focused reading panel. |
| Questions | Uses a mix of detail, purpose, inference, tone and reference questions. |
| Model analysis | Appears after marking: clue in text, model answer and why the strategy works. |

## Design treatment

Junior modules use sky-blue and leaf-green details to feel approachable; senior reading analysis uses the established navy and gold palette to signal more deliberate close reading. Both retain bilingual, English-first instructional labels and responsive layouts.
