# P5–P6 Grammar and S1 Interview Extension Map

This release adds original P5–P6 language practice and interview-preparation activities to the Primary English Studio. It is a local learning resource rather than a standardised Hong Kong secondary-school interview, an official examination, a school assessment or a prediction of admission outcomes.

| Area | P5 focus | P6 focus | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Present perfect | *already*, *yet*, *ever*, *for* and *since*, contrasted with finished past time | present relevance, *already/yet*, *for/since* and completed experiences | Four-option contextual grammar checks with bilingual explanations |
| Complex sentence control | *although*, first conditional, relative clauses, indirect questions and infinitive patterns | past continuous with simple past, passive voice, second conditional, reported speech, gerunds/infinitives and formal connectors | Four-option contextual grammar checks with bilingual explanations |
| S1 Interview listening | personal information, interest, activity and reason | transition experience, responsibility, challenge and practical next step | Original replayable scripts, with objective detail/purpose checks and transcript after checking |
| S1 Interview speaking | self-introduction and a favourite school activity | transition advice and community contribution | Role card, timing, language frames, short model, self-review and browser-local completion record |

## Boundaries

Objective grammar and listening-detail questions provide immediate response feedback. The speaking activities use role cards, language frames and a learner self-check. They do not judge accent, fluency, pronunciation, content quality, school suitability or interview outcome. Speaking plans and completion records remain in the current browser; the site provides no account or upload facility.
