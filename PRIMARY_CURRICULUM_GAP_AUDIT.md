# P1–P6 English Curriculum Coverage Audit

**Purpose.** This audit maps the existing Primary English Studio against the user-provided P1–P6 learning progression. It identifies coverage to retain, targeted gaps to strengthen, and the original interactive resources to add. The material is an in-house learning resource, not an Education Bureau paper, a school examination, or a score prediction.

## Current baseline

The current studio already provides a bilingual P1–P6 scope, vocabulary and grammar practice, reading, listening, sentence building, proofreading, local-draft writing plans, speaking prompts, P1–P3 phonics and word-matching games, P4–P6 advanced reading, and P4–P6 writing models. Its existing expansion layers also provide Hong Kong contexts, high-primary listening vocabulary, quick checks and role-play.

| Area | Existing strengths | Coverage to reinforce in this release |
|---|---|---|
| P1–P2 grammar | Be verbs, articles, pronouns, *can*, simple present, *there is/are*, present continuous and basic time language are already represented. | Explicit present-continuous time clue, capital letter/full stop practice, possession, plural endings, and precise time prepositions. |
| P3–P4 grammar | Simple past, irregular verbs, comparatives, future forms, modals, quantity words and initial present perfect are already represented. | Future plans, movement prepositions, superlatives, *have to*, and connectors including *before*, *after* and *if*. |
| P5–P6 grammar | Present perfect, conditionals, passive voice, relative clauses, reported speech and sentence complexity are already represented. | A more deliberate contrast of completed past time with present relevance, possession/reflexives, pronoun accuracy, and controlled complex-sentence linking. |
| P1–P3 reading | Short narrative, literal detail, simple inference, phonics and listening games are already represented. | Picture-story cues, notices/timetables, sequencing, and short-text form awareness. |
| P4–P6 reading | Information texts, emails, reports, posters, opinion pieces and advanced-reading analysis are already represented. | Context clues, pronoun reference, purpose/audience, procedural texts, diary/letter form, and deliberately varied text forms. |
| P1–P3 writing | Sentence building, proofreading and short writing prompts are already represented. | Explicit punctuation, subject–verb–object review, and 30–50-word picture-sequence scaffolds. |
| P4–P6 writing | High-score models, genre support, sentence patterns and locally saved planners are already represented. | A consolidated skill studio with paragraph map, sentence-expansion prompts and controlled genre practice alongside the existing P4–P6 models. |

## Release design

The new extension will add a **curriculum-coverage layer** rather than replace any existing bank. Each year level will receive twelve core vocabulary items, four grammar checks, three short reading texts with two objective questions each, three sentence-building prompts and three proofreading pairs. Every grade will also receive two guided writing-studio tasks with bilingual directions, an age-appropriate word target, a planning sequence and a self-check stored only in the learner’s current browser.

The reading additions use original, age-appropriate text forms that progress from picture-story cues, labels, notices and timetables in junior primary to diaries, instructions, emails, reports, notices, letters and opinion texts in upper primary. The writing additions progress from sentence and picture-sequence control to paragraphing and purposeful text types. The new material will continue the English-first interface with immediate Traditional Chinese support.

## Boundaries and verification

Objective grammar and reading responses may receive immediate feedback. Writing is a planning and self-check activity only: no automated language-quality score, school record, account, upload or predicted result is produced. The release will add an automated curriculum-coverage check, syntax checks, the existing full-site and Gemini-editing audits, and local plus public browser verification.
