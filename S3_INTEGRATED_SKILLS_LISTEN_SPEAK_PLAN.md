# S3 DSE-Bridge Integrated Skills and Listening-Speaking Plan

## Positioning

The new S3 content is **original DSE-bridge practice for lower-secondary learners**. It is not an official HKDSE paper, official marking scheme or score prediction. HKEAA’s English Language FAQ identifies Paper 3 as Listening and Integrated Skills and notes that word guides support task fulfilment, planning and proofreading. HKEAA also publishes Paper 4 speaking guidelines covering pronunciation and delivery, communication strategies, vocabulary and language patterns, plus ideas and organisation. [1] [2]

| New module | Purpose | Evidence of learning | Assessment boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Integrated Skills Assessment** | Combine source reading, listening input, note selection and audience-purpose choices in one original school-community task. | 8 objective questions with explanations and local accuracy feedback. | Objective answers receive automatic feedback only because they are defined-answer items. |
| **Integrated Response Planner** | Turn source and listening notes into a formal school response. | Source pack, replayable listening input, four-part response plan, language bank and draft self-check. | Completion only; no automated language-quality, content-quality or examination-level score. |
| **Listening-to-Speaking Simulation** | Listen for position, concern and action; then give a structured spoken response. | Two original simulations with replayable input, note targets, speaking task, model, descriptive rubric and self-check. | Self/teacher use only; no automated pronunciation, fluency or speaking score. |

## Assessment design

| Skill focus | Original task evidence | S3 progression |
|---|---|---|
| Source use | Compare a school survey, a parent message and a short programme brief. | Distinguish source detail, audience need, limitation and appropriate action. |
| Listening and notes | Replay a student-council briefing and capture purpose, schedule, concern and next step. | Select relevant detail rather than merely repeat a script. |
| Integrated response | Produce a source-led formal email or short article plan. | Match content, tone and action to a defined audience and purpose. |
| Listening to speaking | Respond to a school or community briefing by summarising evidence, addressing a concern and recommending an action. | Move from a spoken input to a clear, evidence-led response with interaction strategies. |

## Web demonstration: S1 Grammar Quest

The secondary homepage will include a visible **“Try S1 Grammar Quest”** control. Selecting it opens the existing live quest at its first round. Students then select one of four answers and press **“Check phrase / 核對片語”** to see immediate correct/incorrect styling, bilingual explanation, and browser-local objective-practice feedback. No answer is pre-filled or artificially marked as correct.
