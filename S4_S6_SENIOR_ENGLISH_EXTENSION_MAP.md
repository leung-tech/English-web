# S4–S6 Senior English Studio — Extension Map

> **Scope boundary.** The Senior English Studio contains original preparation materials. It does not reproduce HKDSE papers, provide official marking schemes, or predict examination results.

The Education Bureau lists the CDC-HKEAA English Language Curriculum and Assessment Guide for Secondary 4–6, while the HKEAA provides the corresponding English Language curriculum-and-assessment entry and year-specific assessment-framework links.[1] [2] [3] The website uses these official pages only to calibrate a distinct senior-secondary learning layer; the exercises, reading texts, source packs and models are original.

| Senior stage | Skill area | New original module | What students practise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S4 Build** | Advanced grammar | **Advanced Grammar Lab** — 10 items | Inversion, cleft sentences, participle clauses, mixed conditionals, subjunctive recommendation, nominalisation, formal connectors, modifiers, rebuttal and passive reporting. |
| **S4 Build** | Critical reading | **Critical Reading Foundations** — 8 items | Tone, rhetorical reframing, own-words responses, reference, contextual meaning, statement matching, summary-cloze word form and evidence evaluation. |
| **S4 Build** | Writing | **Op-ed Writing Scaffold** | An audience-aware opinion article with a hook, limited evidence, concession, rebuttal and purposeful closing. Practice target: 220–260 words. |
| **S5 Analyse** | Academic grammar | **Academic Grammar Lab** — 10 items | `Only after` inversion, what-clefts, perfect passive participles, conditional inversion, formal demands, nominalisation, advanced connectors, reduced relatives, parallelism and hedging. |
| **S5 Analyse** | Analytical reading | **Analytical Reading Lab** — 8 items | Tone, metaphor, inference, parallelism, statement matching, own-words rephrasing, summary-cloze synonym choice and critical-extension questions. |
| **S5 Analyse** | Writing | **Formal Report Scaffold** | Objective headings, findings, limitation, passive reporting, practical recommendations and review criteria. Practice target: 280–330 words. |
| **S6 Respond** | Precision grammar | **Precision Grammar Lab** — 10 items | Negative and `Not until` inversion, clefts, passive participle clauses, mixed conditionals, subjunctives, academic nominalisation, concessive connectors, formal modifiers and bounded conclusions. |
| **S6 Respond** | Source response | **Source-response Reading** — 8 items | Comparison of two sources, tone, purpose and register, implicit meaning, text matching, synthesis in own words, summary-cloze nominalisation and evidence evaluation. |
| **S6 Respond** | Writing | **Debate Speech Scaffold** | Audience-aware opening, social/psychological/practical reasoning, source use, counterargument, rebuttal, advanced sentence variation and a decisive close. Practice target: 380–420 words. |

## Learning route

Students can use the three levels in sequence. S4 prioritises accurate control and a clear line of argument. S5 requires comparison, formal register and evidence-aware explanation. S6 adds synthesis across sources, qualified conclusions, counterargument and more demanding audience-aware response. Each objective item gives immediate feedback; writing scaffolds save drafts locally but **do not automatically score language quality**.
