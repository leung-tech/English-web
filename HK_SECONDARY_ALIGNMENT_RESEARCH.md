# Hong Kong Secondary English Alignment Research Notes

This working note records the official and government-supported sources reviewed on 22 August 2026 for the S1–S3 English Studio capability audit. It is a curriculum-alignment reference, not an official endorsement of the website or a claim that any individual item reproduces an examination question.

| Source | Relevant finding used for the audit | Design implication for the website |
|---|---|---|
| Education Bureau, [English Language Education – Curriculum Documents](https://www.edb.gov.hk/en/curriculum-development/kla/eng-edu/curriculum-documents.html) | The EDB maintains curriculum documents for English Language Education at primary and secondary levels. | Keep the site organised around progressive language learning rather than an exam-paper imitation. |
| Curriculum Development Council, [English Language Education Key Learning Area Curriculum Guide (Primary 1 – Secondary 6), 2017](https://www.edb.gov.hk/ELEKLACG-2017/default.html) | The guide is the overall English Language Education KLA framework spanning the basic and secondary stages. | Audit each S1–S3 stage for balanced exposure to listening, speaking, reading, writing and language knowledge in meaningful contexts. |
| HKEAA, [HKDSE English Language Curriculum and Assessment Guide page](https://www.hkeaa.edu.hk/en/hkdse/assessment/subject_information/category_a_subjects/hkdse_subj.html?A1&1&2_2) | The HKEAA identifies the English Language Curriculum and Assessment Guide for S4–S6 as the official senior-secondary reference. | Label S3 work only as a **DSE bridge**. | EDB Language Learning Support Section, [Nourishing reflective students into their own assessors through meaningful curriculum integration](https://cd1.edb.hkedcity.net/cd/languagesupport/sbcp_05_2022_compendium_02.html) | The EDB example maps text types, contextual vocabulary, language features, writing skills, tone/style/register, cohesive devices, source material, self-evaluation and progressively more challenging tasks. ## Working alignment standard for this update

The update will treat **S1–S2** as junior-secondary contextual English practice: school, media, community, wellbeing and digital-citizenship scenarios; reading/listening of practical texts; purposeful interaction; controlled grammar; vocabulary selection; and scaffolded short writing. **S3** will extend these skills with source evaluation, evidence limits, cautious claims, audience/register and more formal response planning. This is a progression model informed by the sources above, not an official year-by-year syllabus or examination blueprint.
