# S1–S3 Varied Practice Expansion Plan

The existing studio already provides strong grammar, vocabulary, dialogue and speaking routes. This extension adds more **practice formats** so students can transfer knowledge across skills instead of repeatedly answering only one question type.

| Year | New reading and listening practice | New language-use practice | New writing and review practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Short practical-text reading and school-announcement listening, with key-detail and sequence questions. | Sentence repair for articles, verb forms, question order and polite requests. | Guided email planner and compact four-skill checkpoint. |
| S2 | Paired message reading and meeting-note listening, with claim, purpose and evidence questions. | Editing clinic for linkers, evidence wording, reporting language and balanced claims. | Proposal planner with evidence, concern and review steps. |
| S3 | Source-comparison reading and panel-discussion listening, with inference, limitation and recommendation questions. | Precision editing for hedging, cohesion, source acknowledgement and formal register. | Source-led response planner plus an applied mixed-skill review that connects reading, listening, language use and a writing decision. |

The new content will remain original practice, English-first and bilingual where immediate support is needed. Each task will use existing replay, answer-check and local-progress behaviour. Writing planning remains a self-checking aid; it does not automatically score language quality.
